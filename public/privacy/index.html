FREIGHTCOMPARE FLEET

Privacy Policy for transporters

Last updated: 29 October 2026

FreightCompare is owned and operated by FORUS INTELLIGENCE LABS PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us"). It runs FreightCompare.ai, a platform where shippers in India compare freight rates and book transporters. This policy covers transporters who use the FreightCompare Fleet Android app (com.forus.freightcompare.fleet), the transporter portal at join.freightcompare.ai, or the transporter sections of the FreightCompare app and website. For shippers, see our main Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name or business name, mobile number and email address, used to create your account and sign you in with one-time codes.
  • Business details: for GST-registered businesses, your GST number, business address and office hours.
  • Identity verification (KYC): photos of the front and back of your Aadhaar card and a selfie, used to confirm your identity and protect shippers from fake listings. A face check on the selfie runs on your phone.
  • Vehicle verification: the driver's name and mobile number, and photos of the driving licence and vehicle registration certificate (RC).
  • Documents you upload: for example a rate list, visiting card, RC or a PDF/Excel file, which we read to fill in your details and rates for you.
  • Your routes, rates and vehicles: the lanes you serve, your prices and your fleet.
  • Location: an approximate location when the app opens, to choose your country and currency. Your precise location only if you tap "use my location" while entering an address. You can deny location access and type the address instead.
  • Invite codes: if a shipper or another transporter invited you, the code you used, so we can connect you to them.

This policy is governed by the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

We do not collect your contacts, messages, call logs, health or payment card data, and Fleet shows no ads.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To verify transporters (KYC and vehicle checks) and prevent fraud.
  • To list your business, routes and rates for shippers, and to connect you to shippers who invited you.
  • To fill in forms for you from documents you upload.
  • To send you service messages (one-time codes, invites, account updates) by SMS, WhatsApp or email.

3. Who can see your information

  • Shippers on FreightCompare see your business name, contact number, routes and rates. Shippers who invited you see them straight away, and other shippers only if you choose to list publicly. This is the purpose of the service and happens at your request.
  • Your KYC and vehicle documents (Aadhaar, selfie, driving licence, RC) are never shown to shippers or other users. Only our verification team sees them, to approve your account.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us (cloud hosting, SMS/WhatsApp/email delivery, document reading) process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured servers with access limited to staff who need it. We keep your data while your account is active, and afterwards only as long as the law requires.

5. Your rights and deleting your account

You can see and correct your details in the app. To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and its data (including KYC documents), email support@freightcompare.ai from your registered email or mobile number. We act on deletion requests within 30 days, except records we must keep by law. Step-by-step: Delete your account.

6. Children

FreightCompare Fleet is a business service for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
