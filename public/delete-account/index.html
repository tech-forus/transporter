Delete your FreightCompare Fleet account

This applies to transporter accounts in the FreightCompare Fleet app and the FreightCompare transporter portal, operated by FORUS INTELLIGENCE LABS PRIVATE LIMITED.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@freightcompare.ai with the subject "Delete my FreightCompare Fleet account".
  2. Send it from the email address on your account, or include the mobile number you signed up with, so we can find it.
  3. We may send a one-time code to that mobile number or email to confirm it's really you.
  4. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What we delete

  • Your account: name or business name, mobile number, email address and password.
  • Business details: GST number, address and office hours.
  • KYC and vehicle documents: Aadhaar photos, selfie, driving licence and RC photos, and driver details.
  • Documents you uploaded (rate lists, visiting cards, PDF/Excel files).
  • Your routes, rates and vehicles. Your listing stops being visible to all shippers, including shippers who invited you.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records we must keep by law (for example tax or invoice records, if any), for as long as the law requires, then deleted.
  • Security and fraud-prevention logs for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • A shipper's own records of past bookings with you stay in that shipper's account.

Want to delete only some data and keep your account? Email us and say what to remove. You can also delete your routes yourself in the app.
